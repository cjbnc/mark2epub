# Sausage and Pasta in Cream Sauce

This makes a really tasty cream sauce that's not too heavy. The original
recipe calls for chicken sausage and gnocchi, we made it with kielbasa
and pasta. It would probably work with other meats and broccoli instead
of spinach.

- 8 oz radiatori pasta
- 2 TBSP olive oil
- 16oz kielbasa or other cooked sausage
- 2 shallots, finely chopped
- 2 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 cup white wine
- 1 TBSP cornstarch
- 1/2 cup low sodium chicken broth
- 1/2 package frozen chopped spinach
- 1/2 cup heavy cream
- 1/4 cup shredded parmesan

Cook pasta according to package directions. Meanwhile in a large
skillet, heat oil over medium-high heat. Cook sausage and shallots until
sausage is browned and shallots are tender. Add garlic and cook for one
more minute.

Stir in wine. Bring to a boil, cook until liquid is reduced by half, 3-4 minutes.
In a small bowl, mix cornstarch and broth until smooth. Stir into sausage mixture. 
Return to a boil, stirring constantly. Cook until thickened, 1-2 minutes.
Add spinach and cream. Stir until spinach is warmed and loose.

Drain the pasta and add to the pan. Sprinkle with parmesan and stir together.

Source: Taste of Home - Gnocchi with Chicken Sausage
