# Korean Bone Broth (Seolleongtang)

- about 4lbs of beef marrow bones
- water to fill the pot
- 1-2 lbs of beef brisket or chuck

Soak the bones in cold water for one hour or more to draw out
the blood. Rinse well and drain.

Soak the meat in a separate bowl for one hour as well. Drain 
and refrigerate until needed.

Add bones to a large stock pot (8qt or more) and cover with cold
water. Bring to a boil over high heat. Reduce the heat to medium and
boild for 5 minutes.

Drain. Rinse the bones, and clean out the pot to remove any brown
bits. Return the bones to the pot.

Fill up the pot with cold water, leaving a little room for boiling.
Bring it to a boil over high heat, and reduce the heat to medium.

Cover, and boil until the broth becomes rich and milky, about 5 hours
(or longer if you have time). Adjust the heat a little, if necessary,
to maintain a medium boil. Add more water (boiling hot water to
maintain the cooking temperature) to cover the bones, once or twice
while boiling.

After the first 3-ish hours of boiling the bones, add the soaked meat
(and more water if needed to submerge the meat). Boil until the meat
is tender, for 1-1/2 to 2 hours. Remove the meat. Once cooled, thinly
slice the meat to add to the soup when serving. Pour the broth
through a colander into another pot or a large bowl to cool.

[Optional step for milkier broth] Fill up the pot with fresh water
again. Bring to a boil over high heat. Reduce the heat to medium.
Cover, and boil until the broth becomes rich and milky, 3 - 5 hours.
Reduce the heat a little, as necessary, to maintain a moderate boil.
Pour the milky broth through a colander into the pot or large bowl
that contains the first batch.

Use the broth and shredded meat in another soup recipe, below. Freeze
any extra broth. A single batch made 5-1/2 quarts in my stockpot.

Source: https://www.koreanbapsang.com/seolleongtang-beef-bone-soup/

# Rice Cake and Dumpling Soup (Tteok Mandu Guk)

Makes 2 servings

- 1 quart bone broth from above
- 1 TBSP Dasida (beef boullion powder)
- 1 tsp minced garlic
- 1 egg
- 2 portions of the cooked beef
- 225g (1/2 lb) sliced rice cakes
- 5-6 frozen dumplings
- 1 green onion, finely sliced

Soak the rice cakes in cold water for 20-30 mins. Slice the green onions.

Make the egg garnish - scramble the egg. Cook over medium heat in a small frying pan
without stirring. Flip to cook the other side just until set. Remove from heat
and slice into strips.

Bring the bone broth to a boil. Add the Dasida and garlic. Add the frozen dumplings 
and simmer for about 6 minutes until cooked through. Remove the dumplings to a bowl.
Drain the rice cakes and add to the broth. Simmer for 1-2 minutes until soft.
Stir in most of the green onion and cook for another 30 seconds to soften. 

Split the broth and rice cakes into two bowls. Add the beef, cooked dumplings, and
egg strips. Garnish with the remaining green onion.
