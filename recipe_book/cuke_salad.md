# Cucumber Salads

## Asian Cucumber/Daikon Salad

- 1 cucumber, thinly sliced
- 1 daikon radish, thinly sliced

Salt the veggies and let them drain for 30 minutes. 
Rinse off the extra salt and pat dry.

- 1 tsp minced garlic
- 2 TBSP soy sauce
- 2 TBSP rice wine vinegar
- 1 tsp sesame oil
- 1 tsp sugar
- pinch of chili flakes or cayenne, optional

In a large bowl mix the dressing. Stir in the drained veggies. Put in a
container in the refrigerator for at least an hour before serving.

## Peanut Butter Cucumber Salad

A Korean version from Aaron and Claire.

- 2 English cucumbers
- 2 garlic cloves
- 1 green onion (optional)
- 1 TBSP toasted sesame seeds, ground

Trim the ends off the cucumbers. Peel if desired. Cut into bite-sized 
pieces. Finely chop the garlic, and thinly slice the green onion if 
using.

If the salad is going to be kept for a few days, lightly salt and drain
the cucumber pieces for 30 minutes and then rinse off the extra salt.

- 3 TBSP creamy peanut butter
- 1 TBSP gochugaru (pepper flakes)
- 1 TBSP soy sauce
- 1/2 TBSP sugar
- 2 TBSP lemon juice
- 2 tsp sesame oil


Make the dressing in a small container.

Pour the dressing over the cucumbers. Sprinkle with the ground sesame. 
Gently toss together until evenly coated. Taste and add salt if needed.
