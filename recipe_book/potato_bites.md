# Crispy Potato Pancake Bites

- 2 large russet potatoes
- 3 TBSP butter, melted
- 1 TBSP olive oil
- 1/2 TBSP garlic powder
- 1 tsp kosher salt (or less)
- 1/4 tsp ground black pepper
- 1 TBSP AP flour
- 1/2 cup grated parmesan cheese

Preheat the oven to 450F. Use a __non-stick__ mini-muffin pan with 24
cups. Thoroughly butter the bottom and sides of each muffin cup.
Place the muffin tin a sheet pan for easier handling

Peel the potatoes then grate with a box grater into a bowl of water.
When finished, add water, rinse, and drain the shredded potato until the
water runs clear. Drain throughly. Wrap in a clean kitchen towel and
squeeze out any excess water. Please the shreds in a bowl and fluff them
to separate the pieces. Drizzle with the olive oil.

In another bowl, combine the spices, flour, and cheese and mix lightly.
Sprinle over the potato shreds. Lightly toss until all of the shreads
are coated with the dry mixture.

Distribute the mixture to the muffin pan. After the cup are evenly
filled, clean up the top of the pan by pushing the shreds into the
nearest cup. They should be filled to slightly heaping above the pan.

Bake for 40-45 minutes. Remove fromt he oven and let cool for 5 minutes.
Then flip the pan to release the potato bites. They should come out
easily and all be golden brown on the tops and bottoms.

Serve with sour cream and chives, or a creamy dip, or just eat them.
