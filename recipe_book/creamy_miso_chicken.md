# Creamy Miso Chicken

Marinade:

- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp onion powder
- 1 TBSP olive oil
- salt and pepper, go light on the salt

Miso mixture:

- 1 TBSP miso, or doenjang
- 2 tsp chicken bouillon powder (containing MSG)
- 1 TBSP mirin

Remaining ingredients:

- 1 lb bonesless, skin on chicken thighs, or skinless, or breast meat
- 1/2 lb button mushrooms
- 5 cloves garlic
- 1 shallot, or 1/4 yellow onion
- 3-4 green onions
- 1 TBSP neutral oil
- 1-1/2 cups heavy cream
- 1/2 cup grated Parmesan cheese
- salt and pepper to taste

Marinate the chicken: Place the chicken in a mixing bowl. Add the garlic
powder, onion powder, salt, black pepper, and olive oil. Gently massage
and set aside while you prepare the remaining ingredients.

Prep the vegetables: Quarter the mushrooms. Finely chop the garlic
cloves. Finely dice the shallot. Thinly slice the green onions,
separating the whites and greens.

Make the miso mixture: In a small container, combine the miso, chicken
bouillon powder, and mirin. Mix well and set aside.

Make the dish:

In a large pan, heat the oil over medium-high heat. Once it gets nice
and hot, place the chicken skin-side down in a single layer and sear for
2 minutes or until golden brown. Flip and cook for another 4 to 5
minutes or until cooked through. (If there’s too much fat in the pan,
wipe away the excess with a paper towel.) Remove the chicken from the
pan and set aside.

In the same pan, add the garlic, shallot, and white parts of the green
onions. Saute for 1 minute or until fragrant. Add the butter, mushrooms,
and a pinch of salt. Stir-fry for 5 minutes, or until the mushrooms are
nicely browned.

While the mushrooms are cooking, cube the cooked chicken, if desired.

Add the miso mixture and saute for 1 minute, stirring constantly. Pour
in the heavy cream and stir. Once it starts to bubble, reduce the heat
to medium-low and gently simmer for 4 to 5 minutes.

Add the Parmesan cheese and a few shakes of black pepper. Simmer for 1
minute, or until the cheese is melted and the sauce is smooth. Taste and
adjust with salt if needed.

Return the chicken to the pan along with any resting juices. Toss until
the chicken is evenly coated in the creamy miso sauce.

Garnish with the green parts of the green onions and serve with your
favorite pasta, bread, or rice. Enjoy!
