# Dumpling Soup with Rice Cakes (Ttoek Mandu Guk)

Korean New Years Day Soup

Per person -

- 2-3 cups of white bone broth (unseasoned)
- 1/4 tsp Dasida or beef bouillon powder
- salt to taste
- 2 cloves smashed garlic
- 4-6 frozen dumplings (mandu)
- 150g sliced rice cakes
- 1 egg
- green onion, thinly sliced
- shredded meat from making the broth

Soak the rice cakes in cold water for 10 minutes to soften.

Combine bone broth, Dasida, salt and garlic in a pot an bring to a boil.
Add dumplings and rice cakes. Stir to make sure the rice cakes don't
stick to the pan. Cook until the dumplings float and the rice cakes are
soft, usually 5-8 minutes.

In each serving bowl, split the meat. Scoop out the rice cakes and dumplings,
leaving the broth in the pot

Lightly scramble the egg. Pour into the soup on one side, letting it
form into a cluster. Add half the green onions. Cover and simmer until
cooked, 1 minute.

Ladle soup into serving bowls. Top with remaining green onion. Nori
strips may also be used.
