# Char Siu Pork - Cantonese style

Allow 6 hours or more to marinate, plus about 1 hour to cook.

- 2 lb pork shoulder, cut into long steaks with the grain,
  about 1 inch thick

marinade
  - 1 TBSP garlic salt
  - 4 TBSP brown sugar
  - 2 TBSP oyster sauce
  - 2 TBSP light soy sauce
  - 1 TBSP hoisin sauce
  - 2 TBSP red wine
  - 1 TBSP Shaoxing wine
  - 1 tsp miso or doenjang paste
  - 1 tsp five spice powder

honey glaze
  - 2 TBSP honey
  - 2 tsp water

Use a fork to needle the pork steaks throughly, on both sides. Mix up
the marinade ingredients. Put the meat in a zip top bag. Add marinade
and massage it into the meat. Remove the air from the bag, seal it, and
let it marinate overnight or at least 6-8 hours. No more than 24 hours.
Remove from the fridge for an hour before cooking.

Preheat the oven to 425F. Line a broiling pan with foil and add a baking
rack. Add a few TBSP of water to the pan to produce steam and help
reduce the burning from the drippings. Spread the pork on the rack and
save the marinade for basting.

Bake in stages:
- Cook for 15 minutes, then baste both sides with marinade
- Cook for another 15 minutes, then baste both sides with marinade
- Cook for 10 more minutes, then baste with honey glaze
- Turn the heat up to 450F, cook for 5 more minutes.
- Remove from the oven, baste again with honey glaze, and let rest for 5 minutes.

Slice across the grain. Serve with rice and vegetables.
