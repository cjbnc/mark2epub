# Vaca Frita

Serves 8. About 1 hour 40 minutes.

## Ingredients

- 2 lbs Flank Steak
- 1 White Onion Cubed
- 5 Garlic Cloves Roughly Chopped
- 2 Bay leaves
- 1/2 tsp Dominican Orégano
- 1/4 tsp Cumin
- 1 Freshly Squeezed Orange or 1/4 cup Orange Juice
- 1 Lime
- 2 TBSP Kosher Salt
- 1/2 TBSP Black Pepper
- 1 TBSP Olive Oil

### For Frying

- 3 TBSP Olive Oil
- 6 Garlic Cloves Minced
- 2 Large White Onion Sliced
- Fresh Lime for Garnishing

## Directions

### Marinade

Roughly chop 1 white onion and 5 garlic cloves. Set aside. Then gently
pat dry flank steak and season liberally with kosher salt and black.
Then add Dominican orégano, cumin, olive oil, lime, and the juice of one
freshly squeezed orange. Mix together and let marinate for at least 20
minutes. For best results, marinate for 2 hours or overnight

### Pressure Cooking

Place marinated flank in the pressure cooker along with roughly chopped
onions, garlic, and bay leaves. Close the lid of the pressure cooker and
make sure that the vent is in the "closed' position

Set pressure cook settings on HIGH for 1 hour. Once the pressure cooker
is done, carefully release pressure and remove flank steak.

### Shredding &amp; Frying

Shred flank on a cutting board using two forks or hands. Place shredded
flank steak into a bowl and set aside

In a large saute pan, set flame to medium high and add olive oil. Then
add shredded flank steak and pan fry for 2 minutes at a time before
gently flipping the shredded meat. The goal is to evenly crisp up the
shredded meat on all sides. Repeat 3-4 times until the meat is crispy
throughout. Feel free to add extra olive oil if needed

Once the meat is crispy, create an opening in the center of the pan and
saute the garlic and onions while gently folding in the rest of the
meat. After everything is mixed in, turn off the flame and squeeze fresh
lime juice over top. Sprinkle extra salt if needed. Enjoy vaca frita
with rice, Cuban black beans, soft boiled yuca, or your favorite starch
/ vegetable
